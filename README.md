## 🛣️ Getting Started

To best prepare for Session 1, do these things:

1. 🌡️ Fill out your [starting self-assessment](https://bit.ly/aie11-self-assess)
2. 🛹 Complete your [onboarding call](https://calendly.com/aim-dr-greg/the-aie-onboarding)
3. 🤓 Prepare to hit the ground running in Session 1 by reviewing the information currently available in [`00_Docs`](00_Docs)
4. 🧑‍💻  Work to complete [The AI Engineer Challenge](https://github.com/AI-Maker-Space/The-AI-Engineer-Challenge) and make your first share on LinkedIn!
5. 📖 Start reading the preprint version of [The AI Engineering Bootcamp Book](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1rp7Agndrhn8NSUFDI8a3TNxNwhVrEa47/view?usp=drive_link) - Chapter 10 should help you understand The AI Engineer Challenge even more.

## 📚 Course Materials

[`00_AIE_Quicklinks`](00_AIE_Quicklinks/README.md) is your one-stop shop for all course materials, and our [syllabus and detailed schedule](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1onNOmjOdn7NlWGhzbdU82X7vbd4XdXUoE22i5ymHgcc/edit?tab=t.0) will guide us through the course. Including:

- 📰 Session: The concepts and code you'll need to understand and apply each week's topic.
- ⚔️ Technical Challenge: Put your session learnings into practice by building, shipping, and sharing a application weekly.
- ⏺️ Recordings: In case you miss anything, or want to slow it dooowwwwwnnnn
- 🖼️ Slide Decks: Our slides are your slides; please steal them and put them to good use
- 🗣️ Feedback Forms: The Google Forms that you will use to let us know how we can improve, every class
